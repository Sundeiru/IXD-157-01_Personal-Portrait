the change

preparing for and submitting college applications led me to connect with alumni of the art studio i attended. one of them stood out to me - he was a third or fourth term Product Design student at ArtCenter. his work ethic was quite admirable, and i respected how passionate he was about his work.

because of that student, i got interested in ArtCenter and wanted to go. so when i received my acceptance letter, i was more than enthusiastic to head southward to California. while still in Pasadena touring the school in April, i submitted my enrollment deposit.
