the beginning

the journey began in fields of interactive pixels. in 2018, i stumbled upon an open world video game that presented a level of digital immersion that i had never encountered before: The Legend of Zelda Breath of the Wild.

in 2019, i found Breath of the Wild's official art book in a local bookstore, Creating a Champion. within its pages, the concept art and visual development of the game's characters and environments captivated my interest. thus was a dream born: i wanted to become a video game concept artist.
