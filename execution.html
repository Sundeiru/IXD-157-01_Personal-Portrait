the execution

i had certain expectations about RISD before i enrolled. there was only one unexpected thing that happened to me during my time there: meeting some of the best people in my life. i formed many great friendships at the Christian student club, both RISD and Brown students. during my second semester, i spent much more time on Brown's campus.

the environment of an Ivy League university lit up something inside me. my academic spark got triggered, and i began to be fascinated by the things my friends were learning. before i left, i went on several adventures sneaking into different Brown classes spanning applied computer science, mathematics, chemistry, Christian literature, and Japanese. i found myself so excited to visit the types of classes that art students would run away from.
