the start

i was a freshman in high school during this year. my sister was a junior in high school, and our family began to discuss college with her since she would begin college applications in the near future. my parents turned to me, asking me what i wanted to do.

"i want to do art!" i said. they wondered aloud how i could do art with no formal training. not that i would know, all i knew was the determination i had to become an artist in the video game industry. there was nothing else i could think of besides art. when i thought of university, i couldn't think of anything that genuinely interested me, for i had forgotten my prior interests of coding and robotics.
